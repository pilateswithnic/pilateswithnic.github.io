The small print

A quiet, honest privacy note

This website is intentionally simple. Here's everything you should know — no jargon, no hidden tracking, no cookies.

🍪 No cookies, no tracking

We don't use analytics, ads, or any service that watches what you do. The ambient music, particles, and breathing exercises all live entirely inside your browser and never send data anywhere. Share buttons open Facebook / X directly — nothing is tracked by this site.

📨 Your messages go straight to Nic

Every booking button simply opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to ngardner3000@gmail.com — including the booking assistant above. Nothing passes through a server; your message never touches this website.

☁️ Where this site lives

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load the page, GitHub briefly sees your IP address, just like any website on the internet. GitHub has its own privacy policy. Nic (the data controller) doesn't see who visits.

🔤 About the fonts

We use two typefaces — Fraunces and Space Grotesk — served by fonts.bunny.net, a privacy-friendly European CDN. No Google, no profiling.

🛡️ Your rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK law you can ask to see, correct, or delete any personal information Nic holds about you. Just email or call — details below. The Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) is also available if you ever need independent advice.

🧘 A small note on health

Pilates is gentle and suitable for most people, but it is not medical advice. Please consult your GP or a qualified health professional before starting any new exercise programme, particularly if you are pregnant, recovering from injury, or have a medical condition.
